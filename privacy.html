Privacy Policy

App: Claude Gmail MCP Lucas  |  Last updated: October 13, 2026

What this app is

Claude Gmail MCP Lucas is a personal, single-user tool operated by Lucas Fineschi. It connects to the owner's own Google account (lucas.fineschi@gmail.com) so that the owner can send, read and search his own emails from his own computer using an AI assistant. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users.

Google user data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses Gmail data in the owner's account (messages, drafts, labels and attachments) in order to send, read, search and organize email on the owner's behalf.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the actions the owner requests.
  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.
  • When the owner asks the AI assistant to read or compose an email, the relevant email content is processed by that assistant under its provider's own terms.

Compliance with Google policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local credentials file on the owner's computer removes all stored tokens.
